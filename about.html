Our Story

Galiyat Tours was born out of a shared love for Abbottabad’s evergreen mountains and colonial hill stations. Founded over a decade ago by native mountain trekkers, our journey began with a simple vision: to introduce travelers to the true, untouched soul of Galiyat.

From navigating the historic 1930s Dunga Gali Pipeline Track to camping on wind-swept Thandiani sky ridges, we believe travel should be immersive, respectful of nature, and deeply rewarding. Today, we take pride in having guided over 15,000 travelers across Nathia Gali, Ayubia, and Mushkpuri.

"We don't just organize trips; we share our home and mountain heritage with every traveler who visits Galiyat."

— Founder, Galiyat Tours
