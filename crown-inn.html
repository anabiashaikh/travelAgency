A recently renovated apartment, Crown Inn Hotel Service Apartments offers accommodation in Khaira Gali on main Murree Galiyat Road near Barrian Cantt. Featuring 24-hour room service, this property also provides guests with a scenic sun terrace, pine valley views, and free private parking.

The units come with tiled floors and feature a fully equipped kitchenette with a fridge, a dining area, a flat-screen TV with satellite channels, and a private bathroom with shower and slippers. A balcony with mountain views is offered in every unit. At the apartment complex, units are fitted with bed linen and towels.
