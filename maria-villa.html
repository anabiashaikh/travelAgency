Nestled in the scenic hills of Khaira Gali, Maria Villa offers a premium mountain retreat experience in the heart of Galiyat. Surrounded by lush pine forests and offering breathtaking valley views, this elegantly furnished villa is ideal for families and groups seeking comfort and tranquility.

The villa features spacious rooms with modern interiors, fully equipped kitchen facilities, a cozy lounge area, and expansive outdoor spaces perfect for enjoying the fresh mountain air. Complimentary parking, 24-hour security, and warm hospitality make this an unforgettable stay.
