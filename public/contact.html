Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Galiyat trip.

Galiyat is a year-round destination! May to October offers pleasant cool weather, wildflower alpine meadows, and perfect trekking conditions. December to February transforms Nathia Gali and Mushkpuri into a breathtaking snow paradise with winter snow sports and foggy resort fireplaces.

Galiyat is conveniently located approximately 2.5 to 3 hours drive from Islamabad. You can take the Hazara Motorway (M-15) via Abbottabad or the Murree Expressway via Changla Gali. We provide door-to-door pickup in private 4x4 Prado or Grand Cabins.

Main highways to Nathia Gali and Ayubia are fully paved and accessible by standard cars. However, for high-altitude ridges like Thandiani or off-road trailhead access during winter snow, we provide hill-certified 4x4 Prado and Fortuner vehicles for optimal safety.
