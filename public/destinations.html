Why Visit Galiyat?

Discover what makes Abbottabad's Galiyat belt the ultimate mountain escape.

Mountain Landscapes

Spectacular Himalayan foothills with dramatic cloud carpets, verdant peaks, and breathtaking sunset horizons.

Pine Forests

Ancient dense pine, blue pine, and cedar foliage filling the crisp alpine air with intoxicating natural fragrance.

Hiking Trails

World-famous hiking trails including the iconic Pipeline Track, Mushkpuri Summit, and Miranjani Ridge.

Cool Weather

A refreshing cool mountain refuge from city heat in summer and a magical snow-covered wonderland in winter.

Local Culture

Warm Hazara hospitality, traditional roadside tea spots, wood-fired snacks, and rich colonial heritage.

Adventure Experiences

From soaring chairlifts and alpine stargazing camps to wildlife photography and off-road ridge drives.
