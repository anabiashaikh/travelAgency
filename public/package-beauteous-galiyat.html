Trip Overview

Immerse yourself in the ultimate mountain getaway with our Beauteous Galiyat Package. Designed to combine relaxation, colonial heritage, and thrilling outdoor adventures, this 3-Day tour takes you through the lush pine foliage of Nathia Gali, soaring Ayubia cable chairlifts, carpeted summit meadows of Mushkpuri Peak, and the historic Pipeline Track.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 01
Abbottabad → Nathia Gali

Arrival, Check-in & Nathia Gali Exploration

  • Morning Pickup: Meet your personal driver & guide at Islamabad / Rawalpindi or Abbottabad.
  • Scenic Mountain Drive: Enjoy a comfortable drive through winding pine-covered mountain pass to Nathia Gali.
  • Resort Check-In: Check into your handpicked 3-star pine resort with private balcony mountain views.
  • Colonial Heritage Tour: Stroll through Nathia Gali's historic Governor House estate grounds and wooden bazaar.
  • Evening Walk & Dinner: Relax with evening hot tea, fresh corn on the cob, followed by a delicious trout dinner.
DAY 02
Ayubia → Mushkpuri Peak

Ayubia Chairlift & Mushkpuri Summit Hike

  • Ayubia Chairlift Ride: Soar on the famous open-air cable chairlifts gliding over dense pine forest canopy.
  • Mushkpuri Peak Trek: Embark on a guided 3-hour hike through wild flower meadows to the 2,800m summit peak.
  • Panorama & Cloudscape: Enjoy 360-degree views of Kashmir valley, Jhelum River, and floating sea-of-clouds.
  • Bonfire & BBQ Night: Return to the resort for a warm outdoor bonfire, live music, and fresh chicken BBQ feast.
DAY 03
Dunga Gali → Departure

Pipeline Track Walk & Departure

  • Pipeline Track Stroll: Experience the famous flat 4km pine forest walking trail in Dunga Gali.
  • Organic Mountain Breakfast: Enjoy hot parathas, eggs, and freshly brewed chai amidst tall pine trees.
  • Souvenir Shopping: Pick up handmade Hazara crafts, dry fruits, and pine honey.
  • Return Journey: Comfortable drive back to Islamabad / Abbottabad with cherished mountain memories.

Important Information & Packing Tips

  • What to Wear: Comfortable trekking shoes / sneakers with good grip for Mushkpuri Peak hike. Warm jacket, sweater, and gloves (evenings can get chilly year-round).
  • Travel Documents: Original CNIC / Passport is required at all security check posts in KPK Galiyat belt.
  • Health & Fitness: The Mushkpuri hike is moderate and suitable for beginners, families, and regular walkers.
  • Weather Note: Mountain weather can change quickly. Rain ponchos or compact umbrellas are recommended.
